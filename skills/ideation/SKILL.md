# Ideation

Frame the opportunity, intended effect, and real constraints, then explore
across distinct lenses such as audience, reversal, scale, mechanism, form,
emotion, or analogy. Prefer a small number of meaningfully different concepts
to many cosmetic variations. Make each idea concrete enough to test, sketch, or
reject.

After divergence, cluster alternatives by the tension or promise they expose.
Select a few using stated criteria, risks, and the smallest next experiment;
keep promising unresolved directions visible rather than forcing premature
convergence. Hand selected implementation work to requirements framing or
planning, and use Council only for a genuinely consequential unresolved trade-
off.
