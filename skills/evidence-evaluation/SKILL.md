# Evidence evaluation

Classify the target first: factual claim, interpretation, forecast, or value
judgment. For verifiable claims, trace to the closest primary or accountable
source and test direct support rather than topical association. Assess who made
the claim, when, for which scope, with what method or incentive, and whether
apparently corroborating sources are genuinely independent.

Report a verdict calibrated to the evidence — supported, contradicted, mixed,
or unverified — with the decisive passage/source and important limits. Do not
convert absence of evidence into a disproof or an opinion into a fact. For
medical, legal, financial, safety, or other high-stakes claims, raise the
assurance level and state the limit of this review.
