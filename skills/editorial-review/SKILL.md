# Editorial review

Establish the author's aim, reader, form, and requested pass before judging.
Separate observable effects from recommendations: identify where a reader may
lose orientation, trust, tension, clarity, or voice, then explain why and offer
the smallest useful revision direction. Prioritize high-leverage structural
issues before sentence-level polish.

Protect intentional style, dialect, ambiguity, and risk-taking. Distinguish
developmental, structural, line, and proofing notes; do not turn preferences
into defects or rewrite a voice into generic correctness. Pair with
`evidence-evaluation` when factual support rather than prose is the issue.
