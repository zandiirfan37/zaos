# Creative writing

Begin from the desired reader experience, point of view, voice, and pressure in
the moment. Let concrete choice, sensory detail, rhythm, and implication do
more than explanation. Generate options when exploration helps, then commit to
the version that best serves the work rather than averaging alternatives.

Preserve purposeful irregularity and the author's existing voice. Offer craft
observations and revisions as possibilities, not mechanical rules; avoid
default plot beats, generic uplift, and ornamental language without dramatic or
emotional work. Use `narrative-development` for long-form structure and
continuity, or `editorial-review` for a diagnostic critique.
