# Scientific writing

Keep the contribution, study boundary, evidence, and limitation distinct. Make
each substantive claim traceable to a project result or cited source; qualify
claims that exceed the available evidence and preserve negative, uncertain, or
out-of-scope findings. Methods must describe what was actually done, while
results report observed outcomes without causal or generalization inflation.

Read project-local manuscript, experiment, and literature authority before
changing scientific statements. Use `lit-review` only for its local-corpus,
citation-grounded question-answering scope. A manuscript draft remains a draft:
this skill cannot validate results, choose a scientific method, or promote a
claim without the responsible project's evidence and Human Lead review.
