# Requirements framing

Resolve only the ambiguity that could change the outcome. State the intended
user/result, observable acceptance evidence, material constraints, and explicit
non-goals. Separate facts from assumptions and inspect local authority before
inventing a requirement.

Ask once only when a remaining choice belongs to the Human Lead or would
materially change product, science, cost, or risk. Otherwise make a reversible
assumption visible and proceed. Hand accepted multi-step work to
`implementation-planning`; keep project architecture and project-local
contracts with their existing owners.
